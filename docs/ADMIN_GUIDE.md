# ALFALAH COMMUNITY SUPPORT CENTRE — ADMINISTRATOR & CMS MANUAL

## 1. Accessing the Dashboard
- **URL**: `http://localhost:3000/admin` (or `https://alfalahcsc.com/admin` in production)
- **Initial Super Admin**:
  - Email: `admin@alfalahcsc.com`
  - Password: Configured in environment (`AlfalahAdmin2026!`)

---

## 2. Content Management Modules

### 2.1 Homepage Sections
Administrators can edit each section of the homepage (Hero title, subtitle, CTA buttons, background images, visibility toggle, display ordering) without touching code.

### 2.2 Community Activities & Programmes
1. Navigate to **Activities** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add New Activity**.
3. Fill in title, category (Youth, Sisters, Education, Seniors, Welfare), description, schedule (e.g. Saturdays 10am), and location.
4. Set status to **Published** or save as **Draft**.

### 2.3 Events & Courses
- Manage upcoming dates, venues, recurring schedules, and booking links.
- Expired events automatically transition to past events archive.

### 2.4 Prayer Times & Overrides
- **Calculated Timetable**: Computed automatically for Birmingham, UK.
- **Manual Overrides**: To update prayer/Iqamah times according to the centre's official printed timetable, navigate to **Prayer Times**, choose the date, and enter the official Iqamah times.

### 2.5 Announcements & Notices
- Post urgent community notices with optional pinned status and expiration dates.

### 2.6 Contact Inquiries Inbox
- View incoming messages submitted through the public contact form.
- Mark inquiries as read or archived.
